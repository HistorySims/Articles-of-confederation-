Congress under the Articles of Confederation · 1781–1787

League of Friendship

Thirteen states, one vote each. A bill needs nine of them. Can you pass laws when the people who count are states, not citizens?

Every state gets the same vote. Each dot is about 20,000 free people (1790 census).

Lunchroom Congress

Plan a dance, a trip, and a lunch change for one school. The students stay the same, but each round counts votes a new way: by student, by homeroom, by wing, then 3 of 4 grades.
